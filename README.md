# DFARS Clause Decoder

A free tool that explains defense contract cyber clauses in plain English and tells you which ones apply to your situation.

**Live tool:** https://nehemiah313.github.io/dfars-decoder/

## What it does

- **5-question decoder quiz**: answer whether you handle CUI, have an SPRS score, are a sub, use cloud, and face a CMMC requirement; get your personalized clause list with plain-English notes
- **Plain-English breakdowns** of 8 key clauses: 252.204-7008, 7009, 7012, 7019, 7020, 7021, 252.239-7009, 7010
- Each clause: what it requires, when it applies to you, key deadlines (like the 72-hour incident clock), watch-outs, and related clauses
- **Contract checklist**: mark each clause as in your contract, missing, or N/A; missing ones become your action list, exportable as Markdown
- Everything stays in your browser (localStorage only, nothing uploaded)

## Honest framing

Plain-English summaries, not legal advice. Confirm clause text and applicability with your contracting officer and counsel.
